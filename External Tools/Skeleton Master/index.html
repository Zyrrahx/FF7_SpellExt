Skeleton Master FF7 battle animation slots

Add extra animations to a battle model, with no game files changed: load the model, pick animations, download the two new files.

1Load the model

Choose the model's two files together: the skeleton xxaa and the animations xxda (for Cloud: rtaa and rtda, from battle.lgp or the mod that supplies them). Use the files the game actually loads: a model mod (Ninostyle, for example) has its own.

2Load animations to add

Any xxda (another model's, or this one's own), a limit pack .a00, or a single .anim saved by anim_tool.py extract. The animation must be made for this model's skeleton (the same number of bones). Its weapon track comes with it when the file has one.

How it works

Why this was hard. A party model has 52 body animations: 0-2b its own, 2c-33 the limit pack's. The game finds the weapon track of body animation n at n+34 (hex), so the slots after the 52 are taken by weapon tracks, and a new animation cannot simply be added at the end. The free numbers are 68-9b and d0-ff; each new animation's weapon track goes 34 further on, and every slot in between is filled with the game's own placeholder. The skeleton file's header is updated so the game makes room for all of them. No DLL is needed.

Playing them. In the model's action scripts (the xxab file), a byte from 00 to 8d plays that animation: numbers 68-8d are played by their own byte. Numbers above 8d are played with a7 NN. The table above shows the bytes for each new number. Never use 2c-33 (the limit pack) or 34-67 (weapon tracks) as body animations on a party model.

Enemies have no weapon tracks: their new animations are simply added after the last, up to ff. Models with 16 body + 16 weapon tracks are not handled yet.

Testing. Put the two files (and a changed xxab) in a mod. If the log says "ANM ERROR" or the weapon is missing during a new animation, the files do not match the model the game loads. Keep the originals.

Other tools. Model editors that read the header's second count as "weapon animations" may not understand the new files; the game does.
